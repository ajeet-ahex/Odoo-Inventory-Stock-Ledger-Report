The Problem Odoo Doesn't Solve Out of the Box

In accounting, a ledger shows every transaction affecting a balance — debit, credit, and running total. A stock ledger applies the same principle to your warehouse inventory. Odoo's standard Inventory module gives you current stock levels and basic move reports — but it doesn't give you the full chronological, line-by-line ledger view that auditors, warehouse managers, and business owners actually need.

When your accounts team asks why the closing stock doesn't match purchase orders, or when an audit requires you to trace a discrepancy back through three months of warehouse activity, the native reports leave you piecing it together manually.

The Stock Ledger Report closes that gap. It gives every product in your inventory its own financial-style ledger: opening balance, every IN and OUT transaction with source and destination, and a live running balance after each movement — for any date range you choose.
Quick summary: If you've ever asked "where did my stock go?", "what was my opening inventory last month?", or "why doesn't my closing balance match?" — this module is exactly what you need. It works like a bank passbook for your warehouse.

Key Features

Full Movement History — Not a Snapshot

Every completed stock transaction for any product across any date range, in chronological order — not just what's on hand today.

Automatic Opening Balance Calculation

System calculates the opening stock figure from all movements prior to your chosen start date — going back to the product's first receipt. Never calculate it manually again.

Running Balance After Every Transaction

Each row updates the cumulative stock total so you can pinpoint exactly when a quantity changed and why.

Colour-Coded IN (Green) and OUT (Red)

Incoming and outgoing movements are visually separated — green rows for receipts, red for dispatches — making discrepancy reviews dramatically faster.

Source & Destination on Every Row

Know exactly where stock moved from and to — supplier, customer, warehouse shelf, internal location — all distinguished per row.

Smart Date Filter with Pre-Filled Defaults

The wizard opens with last month's range pre-set. Adjust dates and hit Generate — that's it. No manual configuration.

Only Completed (Done) Moves Included

Draft transfers, cancelled moves, and pending receipts are automatically excluded — every number represents real, physical warehouse activity.

Accessible from Inventory Reporting Menu

Integrated directly under Inventory → Reporting → Stock Ledger — no separate navigation, no extra menus.

Balance Calculation — Transparent & Auditable

Balance = Opening Balance + Incoming − Outgoing

The opening balance is calculated from all stock movements prior to the selected From date — going all the way back to the product's first receipt. Even if you run the report for a single day, the opening balance correctly reflects the entire stock history. Only Done state movements are included.

How to Use It — Step by Step

1

Open Stock Ledger from Inventory Reporting

Navigate to Inventory → Reporting → Stock Ledger. The Date Filter Wizard opens automatically.
2

Set Your Date Range

The wizard pre-fills the previous full calendar month. Adjust the From and To dates to your desired reporting period, then click Generate.
3

Browse the Product List

The filtered product list opens with your date range stored in context. Search or scroll to find the product you want to analyse.
4

Click "View Ledger"

Click the blue View Ledger button on any product row. The system calculates the opening balance and loads all completed movements within your date range.
5

Read and Export Your Full Ledger

The report displays every stock movement with colour-coded IN/OUT indicators and a running Balance column — row by row, for the full period.

Quick Setup Guide

Step 1 — Install the Module

  1. Copy module folder to your custom addons directory
  2. Go to Settings → activate Developer Mode
  3. Go to Apps → Update Apps List
  4. Search "Stock Ledger Report" → click Install

Step 2 — Start Using Immediately

  1. No additional configuration required
  2. Navigate to Inventory → Reporting → Stock Ledger
  3. Set your date range and generate your first ledger
